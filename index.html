The post

A local post of a national Legion.

Post 101 is Lowell's chapter of The American Legion, the veterans organization chartered by Congress in 1919. Here it looks like a hall on Commercial Avenue, a marquee out front, and a calendar built around the community.

  • For veteransA place to connect with others who served, close to home.
  • For families and neighborsBingo, the festival and the hall are open to the town.
  • Downtown LowellRight on E Commercial Ave, easy to find and easy to stop by.
Honor & remembrance

On the parade route, in front of the town.

Post 101 shows up for Lowell's parades with its float and colors, carrying the Legion's mission of honoring those who served into the street where people can see it.
